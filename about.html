ABOUT GISPL

Security is a promise we keep.

For over 15 years, G-Info Technology Solutions has protected the digital assets of banks, hospitals, telecoms and governments across three continents.

WHO WE ARE

An independent security partner, built for trust.

GISPL is a CERT-IN certified information-security consultancy. We assess, certify, operate and investigate — the full lifecycle — for organisations that cannot afford to get security wrong.

We stay deliberately independent of any product vendor, so our advice is measured in your risk reduced, not licences sold. That independence, and a refusal to cut corners, is why clients have stayed with us for over a decade.
