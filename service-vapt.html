OVERVIEW

Testing that proves risk, not just lists it.

Automated scanners tell you what might be wrong. Our testers show you what an attacker could actually do — chaining findings into real business impact, then ranking every issue by exploitability and blast radius so your team fixes what matters first.

Every engagement is scoped to your rules of engagement, run by empanelled specialists, and closed out with a free retest once you have remediated.

METHODOLOGY

A disciplined, repeatable engagement.

01

Scope & rules of engagement

We agree targets, windows, escalation paths and safety limits — in writing — before a single packet is sent.

02

Reconnaissance & mapping

Attack-surface discovery and threat modelling to focus effort where real exposure lives.

03

Exploitation & validation

Manual, chained exploitation to prove impact — no unvalidated scanner noise, no false positives.

04

Reporting & readout

Executive summary plus CVSS-rated technical findings and clear remediation, walked through with your team.

05

Retest & closeout

We re-test remediated findings and issue a closure certificate — proof for auditors, regulators and the board.
