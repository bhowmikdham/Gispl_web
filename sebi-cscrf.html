Services / SEBI CSCRF audit

The CSCRF deadline has passed. Get compliant, properly.

SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework required qualified and mid-size regulated entities to complete their first audit by 30 June 2026. The audit must be performed by a CERT-IN empanelled organisation — GISPL is one.

From scoping to submission.

01

Scope & categorise

We confirm your RE category, systems in scope and the standard formats SEBI expects — agreed in writing before work starts.

02

Audit & evidence

Full-scope assessment against the CSCRF control set by CERT-IN empanelled auditors, with board-level entry and exit meetings per the current CERT-IN guidelines.

03

Remediate with guidance

Findings ranked by regulatory exposure, with practical remediation your team can execute — we stay available while you close them.

04

Report, retest & submit

A submission-ready audit report, retest of closed findings and the evidence trail your compliance officer needs.

Empanelled, and used to regulators.

CERT-IN empanelled

CSCRF audits are valid only from empanelled organisations — roughly 200 firms nationally. GISPL is one of them.

02

BFSI depth

RBI, PCI DSS and ISO 27001 work across banks and financial institutions means we speak your auditor's language.

03

Recurring, not one-off

CSCRF is an annual obligation — half-yearly for qualified stock brokers. We build the evidence base once and keep it current.
