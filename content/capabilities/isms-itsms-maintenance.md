Certification is a project; compliance is a programme. The certificate arrives at the end of a focused effort with a deadline, a consultant and an engaged management team. Then the consultant leaves, the internal owner is reassigned, and the annual cycle the standard requires — risk review, internal audit, management review, corrective action, evidence — slips quietly until the certification body's surveillance letter arrives. The system that existed at the Stage 2 audit exists again for a frantic fortnight before the surveillance visit, and nowhere in between. It provides none of the risk reduction it was built for, and it is one bad audit from suspension.

Maintenance is the programme that prevents this. A named management-system lead from GISPL, on a retainer scaled to the scope, runs the annual cycle against the certification body's dates: risk reviews on schedule, control effectiveness monitored, the risk register and Statement of Applicability updated as the business and the threats change, documents controlled, evidence collected as controls operate rather than reconstructed before the audit. The internal audit programme is delivered by GISPL auditors kept separate from the maintenance lead, so the independence the standard requires is met and documented. Management reviews are prepared, facilitated and minuted. Corrective actions are driven to closure before the auditor arrives. And at surveillance and recertification, we prepare, attend and respond.

## One cycle for every certificate

Most of our maintenance clients hold more than one certificate — ISO 27001 with its cloud, privacy or AI extensions, ISO 20000-1, ISO 22301 — and run them as one integrated system with one cycle: one risk review, one audit programme, one management review, one evidence repository. It is cheaper than three separate scrambles and it produces a system that a regulator inspecting whether the ISMS actually operates — as RBI, SEBI and IRDAI do — can see working. For organisations whose last audit went badly, the engagement starts with the open nonconformities; for those whose management-system lead has just left, it starts the week after.
