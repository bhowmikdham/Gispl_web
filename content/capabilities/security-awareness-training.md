Most breaches begin with a person. Someone clicks the link, enters the password, opens the attachment, follows the instruction from the "director", changes the supplier's bank details. Every technical control in this catalogue sits behind that moment, and none of them is perfect. Security awareness is the discipline of preparing people for the attacks they will actually face — and, just as importantly, of turning them into the earliest detection an organisation has, because a user who reports a phishing email within minutes has done what no gateway did.

Generic annual training does not achieve this, and the evidence is clear: a slide deck everyone clicks through produces completion certificates and unchanged behaviour. What works is specific, role-based and repeated: finance teams trained on payment fraud, executives on the impersonation and whaling attacks that target them, administrators on credential attacks, developers on the vulnerability classes they actually write, handlers of personal data on their DPDP obligations — each in short modules built around real attacks, reinforced by simulation and measured.

## Measured, not assumed

GISPL's programmes begin with an unannounced baseline simulation, so training starts from where people actually are. Simulations continue through PhishSniper™, our AI-driven platform, which generates campaigns that reflect the well-written, targeted lures now in use rather than the misspelt templates of legacy tools — followed immediately by a learning moment for those who clicked and recognition for those who reported, without naming or shaming, because the aim is a culture where reporting is reflexive. Click rate, report rate and time to report are tracked by population and reported quarterly: the human-risk metric a board can follow, and the evidence that ISO 27001, PCI DSS, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and the DPDP Act require. The curriculum changes each quarter with what the numbers show and with the attacks in current use — which is what separates a programme from a compliance artefact.
