ISO/IEC 27701 is the international standard for a privacy information management system. It extends ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002 — the security management system and its controls — with the additional requirements and controls needed to manage personal data properly, in two sets: one for organisations that decide why and how personal data is processed (controllers, or data fiduciaries under India's DPDP Act) and one for organisations that process it on their behalf (processors). It is the first and still the principal certifiable privacy standard, and its controls map, requirement by requirement, to the obligations the GDPR and the DPDP Act impose.

The reason it matters is structural. In most organisations privacy is handled by legal, security by IT, and product decisions by neither, so the gaps fall at the boundaries — a consent mechanism that security never reviewed, a data-retention rule that engineering never implemented, a new feature launched without anyone asking what personal data it collects. 27701 puts privacy inside the management system: privacy risk assessed alongside security risk, privacy impact assessments run before new processing starts, rights handling and breach notification as operated procedures, and the whole thing audited internally and externally on the same cycle as the ISMS.

## GDPR and DPDP, evidenced

No certificate makes an organisation compliant with a law. What certification provides is auditable evidence that the obligations a law imposes are implemented as a managed system — which is what a European customer's due diligence, an Indian data fiduciary's processor assessment, and, ultimately, a regulator or court will weigh. GISPL implements 27701 with a crosswalk to the GDPR article and the DPDP section each control evidences, so the certificate and the legal position say the same thing.

For organisations with ISO 27001, the PIMS is a defined layer on top of the existing system — typically three to five months to the extension audit, with the processing inventory the longest step because most organisations have never mapped their personal data end to end. For those starting from nothing, we build the ISMS and PIMS as one programme.
