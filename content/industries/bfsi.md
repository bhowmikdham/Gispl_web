Indian financial services run on trust that is regulated in detail. The Reserve Bank's Master Direction on Information Technology Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices, in force since April 2024, makes the board directly accountable for cyber risk and prescribes the governance, the controls and the assurance a bank or NBFC must show. SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework does the same for every market intermediary. IRDAI's Information and Cyber Security Guidelines cover insurers. Layer on PCI DSS for card programmes, NPCI's requirements for UPI and the DPDP Act for personal data, and a mid-sized financial institution is answering to five or six rulebooks at once.

GISPL has worked inside that environment since 2012: public-sector banks, cooperative and regional rural banks, NBFCs from Bajaj Finserv to single-city lenders, housing financiers, payment companies and broking houses. We are CERT-In empanelled, which is the credential each of those regulators requires for the assessments they mandate.

## What the regulators actually look for

Every BFSI mandate reduces to the same handful of questions. Is there a governance structure with the board in the loop? Is the estate — including outsourced and cloud — inventoried and risk-assessed? Are the controls in place, tested by an independent empanelled auditor, and are the findings closed? Can you detect an incident, contain it, and report it within the deadline? Is there evidence for all of the above?

Our engagements are built around that common core. One control framework mapped to each regulator's clauses, one testing programme that produces the reports each expects, one evidence repository that survives the audit and the next one.

## Beyond compliance

Compliance is the floor. The threats that actually hurt — account takeover, mule networks, ransomware against core banking, supply-chain compromise through a fintech partner — move faster than any annual audit cycle. The banks we work with longest pair the regulatory programme with continuous testing, 24×7 monitoring from our SOC, incident-response retainers that include the regulatory reporting, and forensic capability for the day something gets through.
