Government systems carry the data and services that citizens cannot opt out of — identity, welfare, tax, health, land, justice — and they face the most capable adversaries there are: nation-state actors whose objectives are espionage, disruption and influence rather than money. The systems themselves were often built years ago on stacks nobody supports, are maintained by whoever won the last tender, and are replaced on timescales that attackers do not respect. And when something goes wrong, it becomes an inquiry, a prosecution or a parliamentary question, and the investigation must produce evidence rather than explanation.

The framework India has built around this runs through CERT-In. Government applications and infrastructure must be audited and cleared by a CERT-In empanelled organisation before hosting and after changes; CERT-In's directions require six-hour incident reporting and 180-day log retention; NCIIPC sets requirements for the systems designated critical information infrastructure; MeitY's and GIGW's guidelines govern e-governance and websites; ISO 27001 is expected of the organisations that operate them; and the DPDP Act now applies to state bodies as data fiduciaries for citizen data, with specific exemptions that need mapping rather than assuming. GISPL has been CERT-In empanelled since 2022 and has delivered assessments, compliance programmes and forensic investigations for more than thirty ministries and departments, fourteen public-sector undertakings and four defence and security organisations.

Security audits and clearances for applications and infrastructure, in the format hosting agencies expect, with retest and certification — because a delayed audit is a delayed public service. Compliance programmes built on ISO 27001 and mapped to CERT-In, NCIIPC, MeitY, GIGW and DPDP obligations, with evidence structured for each. OT and critical-infrastructure security for the power, water, transport and communications systems the public depends on, with the CEA and NCIIPC audit deliverables. Forensic investigation to the standard inquiries and prosecutions require, by examiners who have testified. And 24×7 monitoring and incident response with the six-hour reporting built into the playbooks. Engagements with defence and security organisations are scoped, cleared and staffed accordingly — our team was formally thanked by the US Air Force for reporting a critical vulnerability, which is the kind of reference that sector understands.
