COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you ask.

The questions most enquiries start with. Anything else — write to info@gispl.com and ask.

How quickly will we hear back?+

Within one business day — usually sooner. Your enquiry goes straight to a specialist in the relevant practice, and the first scoping call typically happens the same week.

Will you sign an NDA before we share details?+

Yes, and we encourage it. We routinely sign NDAs before any scoping discussion — note it in the form and we will send ours, or work from yours.

What does a proposal cost?+

Nothing. Scoping and the proposal itself are free and carry no obligation — you get a fixed scope, a fixed price and a clear timeline before you commit to anything.

We are dealing with an incident right now — can you start immediately?+

Yes. Skip the form and call +91 124 401 0000 — the forensics and incident-response team operates 24×7.

Do you work with organisations outside India?+

Yes. Alongside our Gurgaon headquarters and Delhi office we operate from Doha and Maryland, and deliver engagements across three continents — remote-first where that suits you.
