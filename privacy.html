Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · 12 JULY 2026

G-Info Technology Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (“GISPL”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data this website handles, why, and the rights you have — including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”).

What this website collects

No accounts, no tracking cookies, no analytics. This website does not set tracking cookies, run analytics scripts, or profile visitors.

Contact and proposal requests. The contact form composes an email in your own email application, addressed to info@gisconsulting.in. Whatever you choose to send — typically your name, company, contact details and enquiry — travels by email and is used solely to respond to you.

Newsletter. Subscribing likewise sends us an email from your own email application containing the address you want subscribed. We use it only to send you our insights, and you can unsubscribe at any time by writing to the same address.

Job applications. Applications submitted on this website (name, contact details, message and CV) are used only to assess your candidacy and manage the hiring process. Résumés are retained no longer than needed for that purpose; you may request deletion at any time via careers@gisconsulting.in.

Web fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts; your browser requests those font files from Google's servers, which involves transmitting your IP address to Google. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles such requests.

Local storage. The site stores non-identifying content data (such as published job listings and articles) in your browser's local storage so pages load quickly. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Legal basis and retention

We process the personal data described above with your consent, given when you choose to contact us, subscribe, or apply. We retain it only as long as necessary for the purpose you gave it to us — responding to an enquiry, running a hiring process, or sending a newsletter you have subscribed to — or as required by law.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example, email infrastructure), under contractual confidentiality, or where the law requires disclosure.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data; withdraw consent; nominate a person to exercise your rights; and raise a grievance. Write to info@gisconsulting.in and we will respond within the timelines the law prescribes. If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
