Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · 10 SEPTEMBER 2026 · VERSION 2026-09-10

G-Info Technology Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (“GISPL”, “we”, “us”) is a data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) for the personal data this website collects. This policy says what that data is, why we process it, who else touches it, how long we keep it, and how you exercise your rights — including the grievance mechanism the Act requires us to provide.

What this website collects, and why

No accounts, no tracking cookies, no analytics. The public website does not set tracking cookies, run analytics or advertising scripts, or profile visitors. Nothing that identifies you is stored in your browser by the public site; the client-portal demo stores fictional demonstration data only.

Proposal requests and enquiries. When you use the contact form we collect your name, job title, company, work email, phone, location, the service and industry you select and your message, and we ask for your consent before you submit. The details are used solely to respond to your enquiry and prepare a proposal. Where our lead-capture service is configured, the form sends them to it and you receive a reference number; otherwise it hands the request to your own email application, addressed to us.

DPDP readiness checklist. Downloading the checklist asks for your name, work email and company, used only to follow up on your readiness programme. The page says so at the point of collection, and that notice is what we record as the basis for the follow-up.

Insights newsletter. Subscribing records your email address as pending and sends you a confirmation link; we only add you to the list when you click it. Every issue carries an unsubscribe link. When you unsubscribe we keep your address on a suppression list so the next form submission cannot re-subscribe you without your say-so.

Job applications. Applying for a role collects your name, contact details, message and CV, used only to assess your candidacy and manage the hiring process. CVs are stored in access-controlled cloud storage and retained no longer than the process requires. You may ask us to delete an application at any time.

Rights requests and grievances. The grievance form collects your name, email, optional phone, your relationship to us and the details of your request, used only to handle that request and to demonstrate that we did.

Consent records. For each of the above we record the wording you agreed to, the version of this policy in force, the time, and the IP address and browser identifier of the submission. The Act places the burden of proving consent on us; this is how we carry it.

Server logs. Our hosting provider records the IP address, requested page and browser identifier of each request for security and capacity purposes, as every web host does. We do not use these logs to identify or profile visitors.

Web fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts; your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which involves transmitting your IP address to Google. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles such requests.

Legal basis

We process the personal data described above on the basis of your consent, given when you choose to contact us, download a resource, subscribe or apply, and recorded as described. Where you make a rights request or raise a grievance, we process what you send us because the law requires us to handle it. We do not use your data for any purpose other than the one you gave it to us for, and we do not sell it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries, checklist downloads, applications and rights requests are erased automatically three years after they were made, unless the law requires a longer period or you ask us to erase them sooner. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe, after which the address alone remains on the suppression list. Consent records live and die with the submission they belong to.

Who else processes it

We use a small number of service providers, each under contract and each processing on our instructions only: a cloud-hosting provider for the website, its lead-capture service and application storage; and an email-delivery provider for notifications, confirmation links and the newsletter. These providers may process data on servers outside India. We also operate offices in the UAE, the USA, Canada and Australia, and staff there may handle an enquiry that concerns their region. We do not share personal data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How we protect it

Data travels encrypted in transit. Submissions are validated and rate-limited, links in email are signed and single-purpose, stored records are access-controlled and expire automatically, and nothing this website stores can be read back out through it. We are a security practice; we hold ourselves to what we recommend to clients.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who it has been shared with; have it corrected, completed or erased; withdraw consent you have given us; nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot; and raise a grievance about how we have handled your data. The place to do all of this is the grievance redressal page, which gives you a reference number and commits us to a timeline. You may also write to the Grievance Officer directly at the address below.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date and version above. The version recorded against your consent tells you which text you agreed to. Material changes will be highlighted on the website.
