Turn the DPDP Act into an engineering plan, not a legal scare.

The rules are notified and the dates are fixed. We map consent, notice, data-principal rights and breach reporting to your actual systems — and make the evidence audit-ready.

Two milestones. One runway.

13 NOVEMBER 2026

Consent Manager mandate

Every data fiduciary relying on consent must integrate with a registered Consent Manager from this date.

—DAYS REMAINING
13 MAY 2027

Full enforcement

All substantive obligations take effect and the Data Protection Board moves to active enforcement — penalties reach ₹250 crore.

—DAYS REMAINING

Five obligations that touch real systems.

01

Consent & notice

Purpose-specific consent, plain-language notices, and — from November 2026 — integration with registered Consent Managers.

02

Data-principal rights

Access, correction and erasure requests handled inside statutory timelines, with a grievance channel that works.

03

Breach reporting

Notification to the Data Protection Board and affected individuals — your incident-response plan has to produce it.

04

Significant Data Fiduciary duties

If you qualify: a Data Protection Officer, independent audits and periodic impact assessments.

05

Evidence of it all

Policies alone do not survive an inquiry. Logs, records of consent and processing registers do.
