The attack surface traditional testing misses.

Aligned to the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

Prompt injection

Direct and indirect injection through user input, documents and retrieved content.

Jailbreaks & guardrail bypass

Systematic evasion of safety and policy controls, measured — not anecdotal.

RAG & data poisoning

Manipulated knowledge bases and retrieval paths that quietly change model behaviour.

Agent & tool abuse

Excessive agency, tool-call manipulation and privilege escalation in agentic workflows.

Sensitive-data leakage

System-prompt extraction, training-data disclosure and cross-tenant bleed.

AI supply chain

Third-party models, plugins and MCP-style integrations your app now depends on.
